   # Privacy policy – rclone-pi-backup

   This application is used solely by its owner to back up the
   owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

   - It accesses only files it has created itself (Google Drive
     scope "drive.file").
   - No data is collected, shared, sold, or sent to any third party.
   - Data is stored only in the owner's Google Drive.
